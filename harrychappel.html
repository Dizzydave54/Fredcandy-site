Harry Chappel

Harry Chappel is a human being who is known to be the bodyguard of Fred Candy. He is often seen accompanying Fred Candy in various locations and events, ensuring his safety and well-being.

Fighting style

harry Chappel is well-known in the community for his unique and borderline torturous methods of abuse. Some examples include:

  • "The slaps"

    • The slaps are an OG strategy developed and pioneered solely by Harry Chappel himself however it was the popularity of the slaps that enabled him to persue his bullying dreams.
